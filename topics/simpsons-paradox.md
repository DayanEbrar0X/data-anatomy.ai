> Treatment A wins in every group, and loses overall.

## The idea

A trend can appear in every subgroup of the data and reverse when the groups are combined. It happens when a hidden variable is unevenly spread across the groups.

A well-known case comes from UC Berkeley's 1973 admissions data: overall, men appeared to be admitted at a higher rate, but within most departments the difference disappeared or favored women. Women had applied more often to the most competitive departments.

## What the lesson will build

- A small dataset where the overall trend and the group trends disagree
- A groupby that reveals the reversal
- A chart of both views side by side

## Key ideas

- Confounding variables
- Aggregation
- Why to segment before concluding
- Weighted averages
