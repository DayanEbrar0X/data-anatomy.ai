## The idea

The mean is pulled by extreme values; the median isn't. On skewed data like income, house prices or response times, the mean can describe almost nobody.

This lesson explores a skewed dataset the way an analyst would: summary statistics, a histogram, percentiles, and a decision about which number to report.

## What the lesson will build

- A skewed dataset with one extreme value
- Mean, median and percentiles before and after the outlier
- A histogram that shows why the two disagree

## Key ideas

- Skew
- Outliers
- Percentiles (p50, p95, p99)
- Choosing the right summary

## The video

- **Long form:** A row of people with the average marker sliding far to the right when one extreme value arrives, while the median barely moves.
- **Short:** The average moved a mile. The median didn't.
