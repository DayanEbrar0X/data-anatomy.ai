# Refund policy

Refund window: **30 days**. Items must be
unused and in the original box.

Refunds go back to the original payment method
within 5 business days of the return being scanned
at our warehouse. Shipping costs are refunded only
when the item arrived damaged or was not what you
ordered. Gift cards and opened software cannot be
refunded, but they can be exchanged.
