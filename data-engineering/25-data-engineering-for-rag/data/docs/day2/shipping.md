# Shipping

Standard shipping: 3 to 5 business days, free on
orders over $50. Express shipping arrives the next
business day if you order before 2 pm.

We ship to the US, Canada and the UK. Every order
gets a tracking link by email as soon as it leaves
the warehouse. Lost parcels are replaced for free
after 10 business days without a tracking update.
